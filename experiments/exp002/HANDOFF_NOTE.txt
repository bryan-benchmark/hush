Question: can a causal system learn recurring speakers from this broadcast, notice when they disappear, and recognize when they come back?

Model: speechbrain/spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb
Revision: 0f99f2d0ebe89ac095bcc5903c4dd8f72b367286
Not fine-tuned. 192-d embeddings, L2-normalized. Device: CPU. NNPACK disabled (recorded in metadata).
VAD: silero 6.2.3, published defaults.
Match cosine 0.25 is the published SpeechBrain verification threshold. It was not moved.
A cluster is RECURRING from history before the current window: at least 3 appearances separated by gaps over 10 s, span at least 60 s, and at least 6 s of attributed speech.
Continuity uses embedding windows that ended in the trailing 15 s. Silence is not in the denominator.
The return score needs an already-recurring cluster to match again after an absence of at least 30 s.
