Local family-test beds. Mixkit files stay in this folder for the older
sports/noise set. Soft / EDM beds are generated in ohush_live/sounds.py
(no separate downloads, no vocals).

stadium.wav
  Mixkit "Big crowd talking loop" (id 364), about 59 seconds, looped.
  A diffuse crowd, with no chant and no music. The clips titled stadium
  were short cheers, so this talking-crowd loop is the steady bed.

rain.wav
  Mixkit "Long rain ambience" (id 1247), about 5 minutes 35 seconds.

fireplace.wav
  Mixkit "Campfire night wind" (id 1736), about 31 seconds, looped.
  The crackle-only fireplace clips were under 25 seconds.

Generated beds (ohush_live/sounds.py):
  neon / charge / volt / drop — instrumental EDM only (no words):
    neon   progressive house ~128 BPM
    charge big-room ~130 BPM
    volt   techno ~136 BPM
    drop   festival drop ~128 BPM
  hush, ocean, stream, birds, breeze, night, chimes, piano, pad, harp, meadow,
  plus pink, brown, and silence.

Default cover sound is "neon".

Mixkit's free sound-effect license allows use in a project.
