# Next decision

IDENTITY-RETURN-001 is a negative verdict for a return switch built from frozen program similarity.

Acoustic distance to a pre-break cloud, semantic distance to a pre-break language cloud, and reappearance of the pre-break dominant announcer were scored separately on ESPN, Prime, and August Hulu. The oracle path, which is told the commercial start, already misses a majority of returns at 10 seconds on every signal and every service. The one near miss is the ESPN announcer, 5 of 10 operational returns by 10 seconds, and the rule required a majority. The causal path has a clean published departure on 4 of 14 ESPN breaks, 2 of 12 Prime breaks, and 1 of 11 Hulu breaks. Where that departure exists, the acoustic return is still on the order of half a minute to more than a minute.

Do not open a prospective start/return mute experiment on these signals. Do not treat ESPN as a passing sports case. Prime is slower than Hulu on the announcer, and the acoustic and semantic lines are slow on all three. Do not retune the self-distance, the 0.25 speaker match, or the 180 second reference. Those were held fixed, and moving them after this table would be a new experiment wearing this one's name.

The recommended next experiment, not started here, is known-content recognition: a frozen fingerprint or other verified alignment to program audio that was enrolled before the break, with an explicit test that repeated ads and bumpers do not count as the program, and with playback as the fallback whenever that match is absent. A generic similarity threshold is the approach this result retires.

No merge, no mute, no holdout, no new detector in this package.
