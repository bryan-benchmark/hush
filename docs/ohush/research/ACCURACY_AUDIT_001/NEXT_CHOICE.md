# Next choice

Neither EX-A nor EX-B is justified as the next experiment.

The largest measured pile is the 273.0 false program seconds on tapes that never received a speaker pass. Running a new voice model, or a source-aware guard, cannot see those seconds until the existing ECAPA pass exists for them. That extraction is not EX-B, and it is not authorized by this audit.

The pod ranking is real: the top 10 earlier commercials account for 69% of the 432.1 seconds. EX-A would rebuild the library at creative level. The match offset inside each pod was not saved, so this audit cannot tell a contaminated bumper from a genuine ad that happens to resemble the program. Rebuilding the library before that offset exists would spend the experiment on an untested cause.

The justified next measurement, when authorized, is to replay the frozen count-10 queries and keep the alignment offset `best_match` already computes. No new threshold, no new model, and no claim about the interior of a pod until those offsets are on disk.

Playback stays play. Universal v1 stays the baseline. Count 10 stays an ablation.
