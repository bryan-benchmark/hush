# Sound direction 002 — 15 October 2026

No live auto-output was enabled. The gate, the detector, and the vibe library were not changed. No listener sat with these conditions for a week. The papers named in the brief were not re-read.

Enjoying a sound, tolerating it for hours, and wanting it when an advertisement stops are three outcomes. A one-minute rating does not settle any of them. `SOUND_IDENTITY_001` measured the sketches. It did not rank them.

## What is already on the device

When the processed vibe files are present, the sports picker defaults to Wednesday Night and can step to silence, Neon, two more Wednesday Night recordings, three Friends Over recordings, two Cocktail Lounge recordings, Rainy Night, and Stadium. Those files are on this machine. Each choice is one recording. The playhead resumes on the next cover unless the listener picks a different bed. Personal songs are not a source. Nothing varies the music by itself inside a mode.

Neon is a generated 12-second loop. The Wednesday Night files in the vibe manifest are 90 seconds. A football pod runs near two minutes, so Wednesday Night is heard about twice per pod and Neon many more times than that.

The return is still the 250 ms bed fade. Program audio that arrives during that fade is dropped. A favorite chorus and a noise bed are cut off on the same rule.

## Where the six proposed conditions stand

| Condition | What can be played now | What it does not answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silence | A bed, and a 60 s file from 001 | Whether a household keeps it after a week |
| Continuous soft atmosphere | Warm Air and Slow Current, uninstalled | The same |
| One repeating groove | Velvet Beat, Light Shuffle, and Neon | How fast that loop wears out across a game |
| Varied grooves | Several CC0 recordings, chosen by hand | A station that changes without a button |
| Listener's own songs | Not connected | Whether a cut-off chorus is worse than a fresh start |
| Generative instrumental | Not built | Whether variation beats a small playlist |

Silence, atmosphere, and one groove are the 001 files. Variety, personal music, and a generated station are the open cells. Building the generator before anyone has kept a condition for a week would answer a question the listening has not asked.

## The comparison that is still missing

Same marked breaks, same loudness, order rotated, more than one evening. The score is which option is still selected a week later, plus manual returns, skips, volume changes, and interrupted conversation. A 60-second preference is recorded and does not decide.

The first four cells can be run with files already rendered. Personal music waits on a source the project is allowed to play, and on an explicit choice between resuming the song and starting a short piece. The fade question is separate and already has a constraint: the return stays fast enough that the missed program audio is the fade itself, about a quarter of a second, until the gate is changed.

No numerical keep-rate was set. Music is the hypothesis to put next to silence, not the winner. The screening for that hypothesis is `GROOVE_VARIED_001`.
