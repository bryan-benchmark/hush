# NFL75_ACCUMULATE_001

Consecutive holds against a rolling 60 percent count. Both lines were frozen on the two NBC games. October 8 and September 24 are development, scored separately. The 8.285 line is the NBC clear-program ceiling and is diagnostic. The return for every new rule is three seconds at or below zero, the logistic boundary. The reproduced 12.155 consecutive rule still returns at 8.285. Nothing was trained. Speakers were not moved.

These ledgers mark ad breaks only. Music, promos, and halftime are not labeled. Ordinary football here means more than 60 seconds from every break. A return transition is the 15 seconds after a break.

## Verdict

Consecutive holds: **REJECT**.

Accumulated 60 percent count: **REJECT**.

Seconds above 12.155 or 8.285 do not fill an advertisement. A 10-second window during an October 8 ad is 60 percent above 8.285 only 6.3 percent of the time. Ordinary football reaches that bar 0.1 percent of the time. The rates differ, and the typical window still fails the bar on both sides.

The mean of the same window does separate. On October 8 the median window mean is about +4.2 inside ads and about −4.7 in ordinary football. September 24 is +4.5 and −4.8. Return transitions sit in between, near +2. The score is higher through an ad than through a drive. It is not parked above either frozen line.

A 10-second, 60 percent window at 8.285 supports 7 of 23 October breaks within 30 seconds, and 2 of 13 September breaks. A 20-second window at 12.155 supports none.
